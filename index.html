Siakap Terminal

Siakap will open with your Windows account only. Anyone who can use your Windows account can open your sessions and saved passwords. If your Windows account is reset or you move to a new PC, this vault can't be opened, so export a backup from time to time. You can set a master password later in Settings.

Sessions, macros, saved passwords and known hosts are encrypted with this password. There is no way to recover it, so keep it somewhere safe.
